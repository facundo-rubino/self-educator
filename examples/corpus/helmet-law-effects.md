# Mandatory helmet laws reduced measured ridership

Jurisdictions that introduced mandatory helmet laws recorded immediate drops in
counted cyclists that persisted for years. The safety effect per rider is
debated; the deterrent effect on ridership is not.

This cuts against the protected-lane finding: both are framed as safety
interventions, but one grew ridership and the other shrank it. The difference
appears to be whether the intervention removes a hazard or transfers
responsibility for it to the rider.
